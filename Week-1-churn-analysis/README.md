## Week 1: Business Analytics Case Study — Customer Churn Analysis (ABC Communications Ltd, Telco Customer Churn dataset)

**A few key findings:**
- Month-to-month customers churn at ~15x the rate of two-year contract customers
- Fiber optic subscribers churn nearly 2x the company average despite being the premium tier
- Electronic check payers churn at 45%, far above automatic payment methods

**Full workflow:** Business understanding → data inspection → EDA → insights → recommendations, all in Python (pandas, matplotlib, seaborn).
