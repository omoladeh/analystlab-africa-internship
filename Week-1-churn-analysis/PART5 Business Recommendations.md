# RECOMMENDATION #1: EMERGENCY FIBER OPTIC SERVICE QUALITY RECOVERY
### Priority: CRITICAL | Impact: HIGH | Timeline: 60-90 Days

## The Problem
Fiber optic service has **41.89% churn** despite premium pricing ($91.50/month vs DSL $58.09), generating 70% of all customer churn. Something is fundamentally broken.

## The Recommendation

### Phase 1: Root Cause Analysis (Days 1-14)
**Action Items:**
1. **Customer Exit Interviews** (Weeks 1-2)
   - Call 100+ recent Fiber churners
   - Ask: "Why did you leave? What disappointed you?"
   - Document verbatim feedback
   - Identify patterns

2. **Technical Audit** (Weeks 1-2)
   - Measure actual speeds vs. advertised
   - Check network congestion patterns
   - Analyze service outage logs
   - Compare to competitors' performance

3. **Support Ticket Analysis** (Week 1-2)
   - Review complaints: what are people calling about?
   - Measure resolution times
   - Identify common issues

**Expected Output:** Clear identification of #1 issue (likely: speed inconsistency, outages, or support failures)

### Phase 2: Targeted Intervention (Days 15-60)
Based on findings from Phase 1:

**If Speed/Consistency Issue:**
- Upgrade network capacity in high-congestion areas
- Implement quality-of-service (QoS) prioritization
- Cost: $2-5M (infrastructure investment)
- Benefit: Immediate speed improvement, tangible to customers

**If Outage/Reliability Issue:**
- Add redundancy to network paths
- Implement faster failover systems
- Cost: $1-3M (infrastructure)
- Benefit: Reliability messaging to customers

**If Support Issue:**
- Hire/train 50+ technical support staff
- Implement 24/7 support for Fiber
- Create "Fiber VIP" support tier
- Cost: $500K-1M (ongoing annual)
- Benefit: Better resolution, customer satisfaction

### Phase 3: Customer Recovery (Days 45-90)
1. **Communicate Changes**
   - Email: All current Fiber customers about improvements
   - Message: "We've listened. Here's what we fixed."
   - Include: Speed improvement guarantees

2. **Win-Back Campaign**
   - Target recent Fiber churners (last 6-12 months)
   - Offer: "Come back free for 30 days to experience improvements"
   - Follow-up: Personal outreach from retention team
   - Goal: Re-acquire at least 25% of churners

3. **Long-Term Lock-In**
   - Offer 12-month contracts with $15-20/month discount
   - Include: Free tech support for 1 year
   - Message: "We're confident in our service now"

## Success Metrics
| Metric | Target | Timeline |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Fiber churn rate | From 41.89% → 25% | 90 days |
| Customer satisfaction | From ~60% → 80%+ | 90 days |
| Win-back rate | 20-30% of churners | 90 days |
| Revenue protected | $4.5M+ annually | Ongoing |

## Financial Impact
```
If Fiber churn drops from 41.89% to 25%:
- Current Fiber churned: 1,297/year
- New Fiber churned: 774/year (at 25%)
- Customers saved: 523/year
- Revenue protected: $6.0M annually
- Payback period: 6-12 months (vs $2-5M investment)
```

## Implementation Requirements
- Executive sponsor: VP Operations (owns network)
- Budget: $3-7M (Phase 1-2)
- Staff: Network team + 50 support staff (Phase 3)
- Timeline: 90 days to impact, ongoing improvement

# RECOMMENDATION #5: NEW CUSTOMER ONBOARDING EXCELLENCE PROGRAM  
### Priority: CRITICAL | Impact: VERY HIGH | Timeline: 45-60 Days

## The Problem
50% of churn happens in first 6 months. New customer acquisition cost wasted if they leave immediately.

## The Recommendation

### Program: "First 90 Days Matter" Onboarding

**Phase 1: Pre-Activation (Days -7 to 0)**
1. Welcome email series (3 emails)
   - Email 1: "Welcome! We're excited to have you"
   - Email 2: "Tips to optimize your service"
   - Email 3: "Your password reset & account security"

2. Phone call (Days -3 to 0)
   - Confirm installation appointment
   - Answer questions
   - Set expectations
   - Obtain preferences (support channel)

3. Support resources prep
   - Create profile with usage preferences
   - Flag any special needs (elderly customer, business, etc.)
   - Assign "onboarding specialist"

**Phase 2: Activation & Installation (Days 1-3)**
1. Installation excellence
   - Skilled technician (top 25% of team)
   - Extra training on customer interaction
   - Empowered to solve problems on-site
   - Post-install walkthrough with customer

2. Hands-on support
   - Technician verifies everything works
   - Shows customer how to use service
   - Provides written instructions
   - Leaves contact info

3. Follow-up call (Day 1 evening or Day 2 morning)
   - "How was your installation experience?"
   - "Any questions about your new service?"
   - Proactive problem solving
   - Set expectations for next steps

**Phase 3: Early Engagement (Days 4-30)**
1. Proactive outreach
   - Call at Day 7: "How's everything going?"
   - Email at Day 14: "Tips for getting more value"
   - Call at Day 21: "Any issues we should know about?"
   - Email at Day 30: "Month 1 summary"

2. Problem identification & resolution
   - Flag customers with issues early
   - Immediate escalation to technical support
   - Follow-up to ensure resolution
   - Goal: Fix problems BEFORE customer considers leaving

3. Service optimization
   - Help customer understand billing
   - Identify upsell opportunities
   - Suggest optimal settings
   - Build relationship

**Phase 4: Relationship Building (Days 31-90)**
1. Quarterly check-ins
   - Day 45: Business review call
   - Day 75: Engagement assessment
   - Invite to loyalty program

2. Value reinforcement
   - Highlight services customer is using
   - Quantify savings (vs competitors)
   - Show ROI of investment
   - Celebrate milestones (30 days, 60 days, 90 days)

3. Lock-in strategy
   - Day 85: Offer upgrade to annual contract
   - Incentive: 10% discount for commitment
   - Message: "You've invested, let's protect that"
   - Goal: Move out of high-risk period
