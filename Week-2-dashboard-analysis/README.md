# Week 2: Business Intelligence & Interactive Dashboard Development

Business Analytics Case Study for AnalystLab Africa Data Analytics Internship Programme.

## Project: Sample Superstore Sales Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built for a retail Superstore dataset, giving management a real-time view of sales, profit, and performance across products, regions, and customer segments.

**Key figures:**
- Total Sales: $307,800.33
- Total Profit: $24,962.70
- Total Orders: 1,334
- Profit Margin: 8.11%

**Key findings:**
- Furniture generates strong sales but disproportionately weak profit
- Phones and Chairs are the top-performing sub-categories by sales
- Sales peaked in 2016 and declined into 2017
- The Consumer segment drives nearly half (46.8%) of total sales

**Dashboard includes:** 5 KPI cards, 8+ visualizations (bar charts, column charts, line chart, pie chart, map, table), and 2 interactive slicers (Region, Category).

**Tools used:** Microsoft Power BI, Power Query, DAX
